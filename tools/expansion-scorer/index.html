The expansion
Eight criteria, weighted.

Demonstrated value carries the most weight. If the customer can't say what the product did for them, the expansion conversation is a discount conversation.

Pick a framework to see each criterion in its language, plus two framework questions. Scoring weights don't change.

Value demonstrated WEIGHT ×3

The customer can say what the product did for them, in their own metrics.

Usage gaps WEIGHT ×2

There are real usage gaps: features they own but don't use, or seats that would get used.

Adoption depth WEIGHT ×2

Adoption is deep: the right users on the right features, not one power user.

Stakeholder coverage WEIGHT ×2

The champion and the economic buyer are both in the loop, not a single thread.

Commercial signals WEIGHT ×2

Usage is stable or growing, support load is normal, no at-risk signals.

Business case WEIGHT ×2

There's a business case for the expansion: a metric it moves and a number attached.

Decision process WEIGHT ×2

The decision process for the expansion is mapped: who decides, in what order, by when.

Budget WEIGHT ×2

Budget exists for the expansion, or the commercial case is approved.
