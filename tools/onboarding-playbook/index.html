Diagnostic
Four criteria, weighted.

First value carries the most weight. A workflow working in the customer's hands beats a completed training deck every time.

First value defined WEIGHT ×3

You can say what "working" means, in the customer's own words. One workflow, named, not "the platform is live."

Named owner on the customer side WEIGHT ×2

One person inside the account owns the rollout and can say yes to the next step.

30/60-day plan agreed, with dates WEIGHT ×2

The plan is written down, dated, and agreed by both sides. Not a deck you sent once.

Usage measured, not just "training complete" WEIGHT ×2

You can see the workflow being used: runs, records, results. Not just who sat through training.
