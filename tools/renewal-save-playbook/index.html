The signal
Start from the signal.

A save is a diagnosis first. Most saves fail because the CSM treats the symptom (the renewal date) instead of the cause. The plan, the message, and the questions change with the signal, so pick it first.

What is the signal?

One signal at a time. If two are true, pick the one that showed up first. That is usually the cause, and the other is the consequence.

Diagnosis
Four questions, weighted.

The real reason carries the most weight. A save built on the stated reason is a save built on a guess.

Real reason known WEIGHT ×3

Do you know the REAL reason it is at risk, not the stated reason? "Budget" means the value was never proven. "We are consolidating" means someone stopped believing.

Second relationship WEIGHT ×2

Do you have a second relationship in the account? One relationship is a point of failure, not a relationship map. If the only person who knows you is the person who left, you are starting from zero.

Value demonstrated WEIGHT ×2

Is the value demonstrated in the customer's own numbers? Value in your numbers is a claim. Value in their numbers is a fact. If the customer cannot say what it did for them, you have a pitch, not a case.

Decision process mapped WEIGHT ×2

Is the decision process mapped: who signs, by when? A save with no decision process is a conversation with no end.

Red flags
Any one of these ends it.

Check any that apply. A single red flag overrides the score. This is a churn, not a save.
